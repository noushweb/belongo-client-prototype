Spec note · Sponsored listings why: monetization is a future toggle, not built yet — shown here as a placement + labelling reference for dev

The Sponsored card above (Palm Jumeirah Beach Residence) is a one-off design reference, not a live ad system. When this ships: sponsored cards are paid host placements, capped to 1 per page of results, always labelled "Sponsored" on the image (never disguised as a guest-verified or AI-ranked result), and excluded from sort/filter logic — they don't get an unfair rank boost over organic matches. Toggle + inventory rules (max per search, disclosure copy, reporting) are a backend/PM decision for a later phase.

Spec note · Recognition benefits why: participating hosts are surfaced to recognised guests — a ranking rule, not an ad

The gold ✦ benefit lines on two cards above appear only for a signed-in, recognised guest (here Mariam, Ambassador) and only on stays whose host chose to welcome that level. These stays rank higher and are never labelled Sponsored — recognition is earned and free to the host, ads are paid. Guests below the host’s accepted level see the normal card. Full rules in the host settings (h12) and admin (a09).
